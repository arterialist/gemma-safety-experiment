1. Refusal Emergence and Post-Norm Weight Surgery

Residual separability diverges sharply at middle layers (L7 to L8 on 1B, L14 to L16 on 4B), where linear probes and difference-in-means directions exceed 0.92 AUROC. Projecting these directions directly into pre-norm writer matrices fails because Gemma 3 applies RMSNorm with unit-offset gains diag(1 + w) after every sublayer write.

2. Harm Recognition Outlives Refusal Execution

Removing the downstream refusal mechanism does not erase internal harm perception. Linear probes trained solely on clean original activations at t_post transfer zero-shot onto the modified checkpoints before activations enter the edited layers. Conversely, probing the user turn ending t_inst at Layer 0 fails out-of-distribution transfer to HarmfulQA (0.0006 AUROC) by overfitting to trailing question-mark punctuation.
